# Session identity

Give a session a color and an emoji so you can spot it at a glance.

1. Right-click a session and choose Color & emoji. It is also in the `Cmd+K` palette.
2. Pick a color, an emoji, or both.
3. Choose Clear to remove both.

The color is drawn as a thin bar on the leading edge of the session's header or row. It is separate from the group dot and the status dot. The emoji appears before the session name everywhere, including notifications and the phone view.

The [phone view](../mobile/) shows identity but cannot edit it.

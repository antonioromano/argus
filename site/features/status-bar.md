# Status bar

A thin bar at the bottom of the window shows:

- **Connection:** Connected, Reconnecting..., Daemon down or Daemon outdated.
- **"N waiting - N running":** click to open the [waiting inbox](../waiting-inbox/).
- **Update available** notice.
- **Keep awake:** click to choose a duration.
- **"Remote: on/off":** click to open Remote access settings ([Remote access](../remote/)).
- An optional clock and version.

Turn it on or off in Settings, Theme & layout, Status bar. When it is off, the update notice returns to the toolbar.

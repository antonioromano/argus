# Broadcast input

Send one line, such as `/compact`, "rebase on main" or `y`, to several sessions at once. You can optionally follow it with Enter.

Choose targets in Find & Jump, or mark them with Space in the [waiting inbox](../waiting-inbox/) and press `B`. A confirmation step shows the exact text and every target before anything is sent.

# Mosaic

The mosaic is the grid view. Each session is a live terminal tile, and Argus shows up to 12 at once.

- Sessions are grouped by working folder.
- Drag the handles to reorder groups and sessions. The order persists across launches.
- Collapsed sessions appear as chips at the top and keep showing live status.
- Click a tile to open it in [Focus mode](../focus/).

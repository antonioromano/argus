# Notifications

- **Desktop notifications** fire when a session starts waiting while Argus is not focused. Click one to focus the app.
- **Tab title badge:** the title shows `(N) Argus` with the number of sessions awaiting input.
- **Waiting count badge** on the Sessions tab.
- **Auto-dismiss:** a notification closes when the session leaves `waiting` or is deleted.
- Turn notifications on or off in Settings.

The packaged build delivers through a bundled terminal-notifier. macOS asks once to allow notifications from "terminal-notifier". Accept, and keep that entry enabled in System Settings, Notifications.

A session's [color and emoji](../session-identity/) appear in its notifications.

# Activity timeline

A live feed of what the agent does: prompts, tool calls with their command or file, subagents and compactions.

## How to use

Focus a session, then click Activity in the header.

## Before you turn it on

- Claude only.
- Only new or restarted sessions are tracked (side effect: existing sessions show nothing until restarted).
- Shows the last 500 events and is not saved.

## Enable it

Settings, Beta features, then switch on "Activity timeline".

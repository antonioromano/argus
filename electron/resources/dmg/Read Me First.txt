Installing Argus
================

1. Drag Argus onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open Argus once from Applications. macOS blocks it the first time
   ("Apple could not verify Argus is free of malware"). Click Done.

3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to
   "Argus was blocked to protect your Mac" and click Open Anyway.
   Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

Argus opens normally after that. Right-click > Open does not skip this
on macOS 15 and later.

Prefer Terminal? xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Argus.app
Installing with Homebrew skips all of this: brew install --cask argus

More: https://antonioromano.github.io/argus/install/#first-launch
